About AMLRO

AMLRO (Active Machine Learning Reaction Optimizer) is an AI-powered platform designed to optimize reaction conditions efficiently using active learning algorithms. It assists chemists in discovering the best experimental conditions with minimal amount of reactions.

Key Features:

  • 📊 Multi-objective optimization
  • 🤖 AI-driven active learning
  • 🧪 Open-Loop reaction optimization
  • ⚡ Fast optimal reaction design
  • 📁 Supports historical reaction data
